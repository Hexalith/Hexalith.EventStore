## P2 obligations retained

The adopted [P2 handoff](../../../../projects/_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-6-1-p2-supply-supported-query-security-projection-capabilities.md) owns complete query-security/projection qualification. `DelegationId` derives only from authenticated RFC 8693 `act.sub`; malformed/absent evidence remains unknown. Projection watermark is the highest positive committed `GlobalPosition` in the event slice durably applied to that read model. Gaps are valid and convey no contiguous global-consumption claim. Positive watermark cursor bindings use invariant decimal formatting.

Preserve opt-in indistinguishable safe-404 behavior for forbidden, cross-tenant, and both not-found forms; non-opted routes keep existing semantics. Duplicate delivery, gaps, rebuild, and restart must converge state and persisted watermark. P1R source authority/wire tests do not close P2's independent authenticated persisted denial/external 404-equivalence, G-4, exact published pin, owner, or rollback gates.
