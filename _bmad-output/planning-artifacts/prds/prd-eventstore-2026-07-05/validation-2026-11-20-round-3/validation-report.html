High

The first readiness seal has no defined inputs

PRD §11.4 and OR1, lines 723, 743, 747 and 809

G-HIGH-RISK needs authenticated evidence for the readiness transition. G-READINESS consumes a passing G-HIGH-RISK result. The transition verifier checks prerequisites before issuing the evaluation that can later be authenticated. Starting with 15 passing business and coverage gates, the document never identifies an evaluation that can produce the first seal.

Correction. Define the provisional evaluation, its exact inputs, and the artifact the assurance check can authenticate. Specify how the final writer consumes all 16 results. Test that the first valid decision can complete without a prior readiness seal. Transition evaluator · Risk matrix

Done-ness clarity

Adequate

High

Story 5.3 permits a Production configuration the PRD rejects

PRD §6.5 and NFR3; Epic Story 5.3, lines 4476 and 4485–4493

The story calls its own acceptance criteria authoritative and permits the symmetric-key override “outside Development.” Production falls within that wording. The ratified NFR, frozen Story 5.3 specification and shared JWT implementation all require Production startup to fail.

Correction. Give Production its own unconditional startup-refusal case. Exercise the override in an explicitly permitted non-Production environment, with its normal issuer, audience, signature and lifetime checks. Frozen JWT specification · Shared JWT validator

High

An accepted command can be reported to the caller as rejected

PRD UJ2, FR12 and FR12-C3; Epic Story 2.15 at line 2182

The PRD permits safe rejection after command acceptance when the response lacks a usable MessageId. The frozen Story 2.15 contract preserves HTTP 202 and Retry-After: 1 and omits Location. The epic also allows a caller’s version claim to suppress the link even when the declared version and the returned ID yield a valid URI. Either outcome can mislead a caller about a successful submission.

Correction. For accepted commands, require 202 and preserve Retry-After. Omit the link for a missing or invalid identity or unusable declaration. Ignore caller version claims and use the endpoint’s declared grammar for valid IDs. Keep submission failures as a separate case. Frozen status-link contract · Generated response tests

Medium

PRD UJ2 at line 193 and §8.2 at line 494; NFR2, FR12-C2 and Story 2.14

Correction. Limit the one-tenant requirement to tenant-scoped requests. Describe the authorized platform scope and its rejection of mixed scopes alongside it. Authorized scope cases
