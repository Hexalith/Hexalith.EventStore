## Residual and new findings

### High · Story 5.3 still permits two readings of Production startup

The story describes its complete acceptance criteria as the authoritative input for fresh planning at epic line 4476. Lines 4485–4493 then say startup “outside Development” fails “unless” the flag permits symmetric signing. Calling this “non-production/trusted-deployment behavior” leaves open whether a Production process may start in a nonconforming posture. The September 8 ratification and frozen spec explicitly forbid it. The shared validator and Production/Staging regression tests distinguish the two environments. **Correction.** Require the Production host to refuse symmetric signing with either flag value. Use a separate eligible Staging scenario for the explicit HS256 exception, audit signal and all other validation checks. [Ratification, line 47](../../../../../_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/sprint-change-proposal-2026-09-08-nfr3-nfr4-authentication-ratification.md) · [Frozen Story 5.3, line 33](../../../../../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-5-3-production-authentication-guards-and-secret-stripping.md) · [Validator, lines 129–138](../../../../../src/Hexalith.EventStore.ServiceDefaults/Authentication/JwtBearerAuthenticationContract.cs) · [Regression cases, lines 161–208](../../../../../tests/Hexalith.EventStore.Server.Tests/Authentication/EventStoreAuthenticationOptionsTests.cs)

### High · An accepted command may appear rejected under the written status-link rule

UJ2 at PRD line 193, FR12 at 284 and FR12-C3 at 441 permit a “safe rejection” for a missing or invalid response identifier. Once the gateway has accepted the command, the frozen Story 2.15 matrix requires the controller to preserve HTTP 202 and `Retry-After: 1`, omitting only the unusable link. Reporting rejection after acceptance could prompt a client to retry an already accepted command. Epic line 2182 also treats a caller-selected version like an absent declaration. The compiled regression specifies a positive case. An endpoint declared v1 with a valid legacy message ID still emits a link when a caller header or query claims v2. **Correction.** Spell out both cases in FR12, FR12-C3 and Story 2.15. Preserve the accepted response when a usable key or trusted version is absent. Ignore a caller's version claim and evaluate the trusted declaration. A genuine submission failure retains its own error. [Frozen spec, lines 31 and 38–45](../../../../../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-2-15-messageid-only-command-status-identity.md) · [Generator, lines 287–303](../../../../../src/Hexalith.EventStore.RestApi.Generators/RestApiControllerEmitter.cs) · [Compiled regression, lines 118–133 and 160–179](../../../../../tests/Hexalith.EventStore.RestApi.Generators.Tests/RestApiGeneratedControllerErrorSemanticsTests.StatusIdentity.cs)

### Medium · The generated API journey omits tenant-free platform operations

UJ2 at PRD line 193 instructs the generated host to resolve exactly one tenant before routing. The cross-cutting §8.2 instruction at line 494 repeats that rule without an explicit request-scope qualifier. NFR2 at 400, FR12-C2 at 440 and Story 2.14 require a cataloged platform operation to authorize its operation and credential without supplying a tenant. Applying the journey literally rejects legitimate platform operations or encourages inventing a tenant. **Correction.** Scope the one-tenant rule to tenant-scoped requests and state what the platform-scoped path admits. Continue to reject mixed scopes before downstream work. [Story 2.14 decision matrix, line 40](../../../../../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-2-14-canonical-tenant-boundary.md) · [Epic Story 2.14, line 2134](../../../../../_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md)
