# PRD Quality Review — eventstore Phase 4 Implementation Readiness Recovery

Validation pass 2026-09-08 against `.claude/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`. Subject: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prd.md` (frontmatter `status: final`, `updated: 2026-08-16`; body lines 55-464). No addendum. Calibrated as a brownfield, chain-top, launch-stakes developer-platform capability spec that is the authoritative FR/NFR source for Epics 4-7 now executing (sprint-status: epic-4 and epic-5 `in-progress`, epic-6 and epic-7 `backlog`).

## Overall verdict

The PRD's skeleton still holds: decisions are stated as decisions (§1.1 OQ8 authority order, §9.3 Epic 8 gating, §11.3 no-authority boundary), scope boundaries are explicit, IDs are stable, and the September rewrite of NFR3/NFR4 is the best-bounded NFR text in the document. What is at risk is done-ness and truthfulness of self-description: every rubric deferral logged on 2026-07-16 and 2026-07-19 is still open, the PRD asserts "no open questions" while owing them, the success metrics are almost all already satisfied so the PRD can no longer say whether the remaining four epics succeed, and the unlogged NFR3 rewrite is simultaneously looser than the shipped implementation on Production break-glass and out of sync with the epics.md inventory that stories are cut from. For a PRD feeding in-flight security and correctness epics, those are high-severity gaps even though each is a small edit.

## Decision-readiness — adequate

The real decisions are visible and unsmoothed. §1.1 (lines 76-83) binds Stories 4.9-4.15 to a hashed OQ8 design and says earlier FR27/NFR7/NFR16 wording "cannot weaken" it; §9.3 (lines 345-349) gates Epic 8 story-by-story; §11.3 (line 442) denies release, deployment, Git, and submodule authority and requires a separate durable release-owner record for Story 3.14. §2 line 91 names the trade-off honestly ("A domain-centric SDK without security ... would be easier to adopt but unsafe to operate").

Where it dodges is in what it claims is closed. §12 (line 460) says "No PRD-level ownership or MVP-scope questions are open" and §13 (line 464) records no assumptions, yet the run memlog records eight deferrals to "the next major PRD revision" that nobody has applied and that the PRD itself never mentions. §1 line 74 ("Implementation should not resume as a full Phase 4 package until ... implementation readiness is re-run") and §11.3 line 440 ("The remaining readiness gate is verification: re-run implementation readiness") read as if the gate is still pending, while the frontmatter lists `implementation-readiness-report-2026-08-01.md` (line 50) and two epics are in progress. A decision-maker reading only the PRD would conclude that implementation is still blocked and that nothing is owed.

### Findings

- **[high]** "No open questions" contradicts eight logged, unapplied deferrals (§12 line 460; §13 line 464) — The memlog (2026-07-16, 2026-07-19) deferred: clause-level completion evidence for omnibus FR26/FR33/FR34; finite bounds for NFR5/NFR7/NFR8/NFR17; correctness-gate binding; product-bet success metrics; glossary and cross-reference expansion (G5, goldens explicitly); and the front-loaded-scope restructuring. Checked against lines 219-244, 274-286, 117-130, and 351-370: none has landed. The PRD gives its readers no signal that any of this is owed, by whom, or on what trigger. *Fix:* Add an "Owed refinements" list under §12 with owner and trigger for each item, or `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts at FR26/FR33/FR34, NFR5/NFR8/NFR17, and §10.
- **[medium]** Resume gate reads as still pending (§1 line 74; §11.3 line 440) — Readiness was re-run on 2026-08-01 (frontmatter line 50) and Epics 4 and 5 are `in-progress`. The stale wording makes the PRD's own gate un-actionable: a reader cannot tell whether implementation is authorized. *Fix:* Restate as "Readiness re-run passed on 2026-08-01; the gate re-opens when ..." and name the re-trigger (new FR/NFR, story split, authority change).

## Substance over theater — strong

Nothing here is furniture. There are no personas, only six user groups (§3.1) that each map to a feature section; the vision (§2) is product-specific to the sentence level (DAPR-native, domain-centric SDK, generated REST in dedicated hosts, projection-confirmed success); every NFR carries EventStore-specific nouns (`MessageId`, `SequenceNumber`, `openbao`, `pdenc-v2`, `501`, `ConfigureAwait(false)`). The rewritten NFR3/NFR4 (lines 272-273) are concrete rather than boilerplate: named option, named algorithm, fixed skew, enumerated secret kinds, enumerated injection channels.

### Findings

- **[low]** §5 Product Concerns (lines 132-144) restates §6 section descriptions and the §7 NFR list — No decision or downstream extraction depends on it; it is a summary masquerading as a section. *Fix:* Fold into §2 or delete; keep §6/§7 as the single source.

## Strategic coherence — adequate

The thesis is explicit and the feature sections serve it: §2 line 91 bets that reuse and hardening must ship together, §6.1-6.2 carry reuse, §6.3-6.7 carry hardening, §9.2 refuses the tempting cheats (moving controllers into UI hosts, treating `202` as success). Counter-metrics exist (SM-C1..C3) and are paired to the metrics they guard.

The gap, flagged on 2026-07-16 and still open, is that the success metrics measure planning artifacts, not the bet. SM1, SM2, SM3, and SM5 were satisfied by the 2026-08-01 readiness re-run; SM4 by the 07-15/08-01 restructurings; SM6 by Story 1.20 (`done`). Only SM7, which is post-MVP, is still live. With Epics 4-7 executing, the PRD has no metric that would tell anyone whether Phase 4 delivered platform reuse or operational hardening.

### Findings

- **[high]** Success metrics are exhausted before the work is (§10 lines 353-364) — SM1-SM6 are already met and are all "artifact exists / mapping exists / packet approved" checks. Nothing measures the §2 line 91 bet: no metric for boilerplate removed from Sample/Tenants (FR9), zero per-message controllers in UI hosts (FR13/NFR14), fail-closed surface coverage (NFR1), or persisted-evidence test share (NFR16/SM-C2). The "product-bet metrics" refinement was deferred 2026-07-16 and is now blocking in effect: four epics have no PRD-level success definition. *Fix:* Add outcome metrics such as SM8 "Sample and Tenants contain zero platform-boilerplate types per the FR9 guardrail scan" and SM9 "100% of NFR1 surfaces have a fail-closed negative test in the release gate", each with a counter-metric.
- **[low]** "All seven Phase 4 epics currently listed in `epics.md`" (§9.1 line 325) — `epics.md` lists eight; the eighth is post-MVP per §9.3, so the sentence is true only with knowledge the reader must bring. *Fix:* "Epics 1-7 as listed in `epics.md`; Epic 8 is post-MVP (§9.3)."

## Done-ness clarity — thin

This is the dimension the story pipeline leans on, and it is where the PRD is weakest for its stakes. The per-section "Done evidence" lines are a good device, but for the three omnibus FRs they cover a minority of the clauses, and several NFRs still state a property ("bounded", "meaningful", "targets") without a bound. The September NFR3/NFR4 text is a real improvement (a named option, HS256 only, a nonempty asymmetric allowlist, HTTPS metadata, 60-second skew, enumerated secret kinds and injection channels) and is consistent with §8.2 lines 305-308 and NFR1's probe exception — but its environment scope is wider than what was implemented and what Story 5.3's acceptance criteria say, which matters because the PRD is the authority that a future change would cite.

Specific verification of the new NFR3/NFR4 against the codebase: `src/Hexalith.EventStore.ServiceDefaults/Authentication/JwtBearerAuthenticationContract.cs` has `ClockSkew = TimeSpan.FromMinutes(1)` (line 160, matches "60 seconds"), enforces `AllowedAlgorithms` nonempty/asymmetric in authority mode (lines 98-109, 228-237), and rejects `SigningKey` in Production "including when AllowInsecureSymmetricKey is true" (lines 118-122). That last rule is stricter than NFR3.

### Findings

- **[high]** NFR3 permits Production break-glass that the implementation and Story 5.3 forbid (§7 line 272) — "Authentication outside Development must reject symmetric-key mode unless explicitly enabled by the narrowly scoped `AllowInsecureSymmetricKey` break-glass option" admits symmetric mode in Production when the flag is set. The shipped contract fails validation in Production regardless of the flag (`JwtBearerAuthenticationContract.cs:118-122`), and Story 5.3 AC describes the override as "non-production/trusted-deployment behavior rather than a conforming production posture" (`epics.md` line 3829). As the authoritative NFR, this text would justify a later change that re-enables HS256 in Production. *Fix:* "Production must always reject symmetric-key mode. Non-Development, non-Production environments may accept it only when `AllowInsecureSymmetricKey` is explicitly enabled, and then only HS256 ..."
- **[high]** Omnibus FR26/FR33/FR34 still have no per-clause done consequence (§6.5 line 219 vs 223; §6.6 line 231 vs 233; §6.7 line 241 vs 244) — FR26 lists nine remediations; its done evidence covers four (no consequence for "clear staged state on infrastructure failure", "tenant-filter parity", "gate admin Swagger", "destructive CLI confirmation", "ULID-safe admin correlation middleware", "stale test-baseline documentation"). FR34 lists eleven; done evidence covers four (nothing for at-least-once documentation, poison/dead-letter, "bound in-memory deduplication", claims normalization, OpenBao, readiness/app-health). FR33's done evidence is spec-existence only, with no consequence for "validate event metadata identity components" or the cancellation seams. Epic 5 is in progress with 5.2 and 5.3 `done`; a story can now close against FR26 with no PRD statement of which clauses it retires. Deferred 2026-07-16; blocking in effect. *Fix:* Either split into sub-IDs (FR26a-i, FR33a-f, FR34a-k) with one testable consequence each, or add a clause-to-story-to-evidence table in §11.
- **[medium]** NFR5, NFR8, NFR17 remain adjective-bounded (§7 lines 274, 277, 286) — NFR5 "must remain bounded and metadata-only" and FR16 line 178 "bounded metadata" name no byte or field bound (Story 2.8 is `done`, so a real bound exists and can be lifted). NFR8 "bounded cost model" and "avoid unnecessary full-stream replay" have no bound and no test; the bound is legitimately spec-first under AD-13/§6.6, but the PRD does not say the bound is owed by `spec-projection-cost-sequence-guard.md`. NFR17 "resiliency targets" names no policy set. NFR7 (line 276) is now acceptable: §1.1 binds it to the hashed OQ8 design and NFR16 line 285 enumerates its proof dimensions. Deferred 2026-07-16; still open. *Fix:* NFR5: state the max detail-payload size and allowed field set from Story 2.8. NFR8: "bound defined by the approved spec at `<path>`; until approved, no Epic 6 implementation story may start." NFR17: name the DAPR resiliency policy set (timeouts, retries, circuit breakers) and the components they must target.
- **[medium]** Unbounded verbs with no consequence — FR16 line 178 "DAPR notification support where needed" (who decides need); FR33 line 231 "reduce projection replay cost" (by how much, measured how) and "validate event metadata identity components" (which components, what failure); FR34 line 241 "restore meaningful IntegrationTests CI coverage" (meaningful by what test); FR5 line 158 "or an approved equivalent" (approver unnamed); NFR12 line 281 "such as" leaves the compatibility set open; NFR19 line 288 "zeroed when no longer needed" has no observable point. FR9 line 162 "removed or reduced" is mitigated by the §6.1 guardrail evidence and is acceptable. *Fix:* Replace each with a named condition: e.g., FR34 "IntegrationTests lane runs on every push to main and asserts persisted state for at least the FR23/FR27/FR30 paths"; FR33 "replay of an already-current projection performs zero event reads".
- **[low]** NFR3/NFR4 altitude and undefined terms (§7 lines 272-273) — NFR3 names the `AllowInsecureSymmetricKey` option but not the `AllowedAlgorithms` option it also constrains; "role, and tenant validation remain mandatory" is not defined anywhere (§8.2 covers tenant-before-disclosure but not role validation); NFR4 "clearly named Development configuration" and "ephemeral developer tooling" are categories a scanner cannot enumerate. *Fix:* Either name both options or neither; point "role and tenant validation" at §8.2 and NFR2; enumerate the approved injection channels as a closed list.

## Scope honesty — adequate

Non-goals do real work: §9.2 (lines 335-343) names what GDPR-1 excludes, distinguishes in-scope erasure (FR5/Story 1.14) from out-of-scope tombstoning, and explicitly refuses the two cheats a team under pressure would reach for. §9.3 separates committed post-MVP from MVP. §6.8 line 254 and §11.3 line 442 say precisely what Story 1.20 does not cover and what nothing in this PRD authorizes.

What is not honest is the PRD's self-report. The deferrals are recorded only in the memlog (see Decision-readiness), and the NFR3/NFR4 rewrite entered a `final` PRD with no memlog entry, no frontmatter bump, and no source proposal in `source_artifacts` — the document's own provenance mechanism was bypassed. Open-items density is zero on paper (0 Open Questions, 0 `[ASSUMPTION]`, 0 `[NOTE FOR PM]`) for a launch-stakes PRD, which is only credible if nothing is owed; the memlog shows otherwise.

### Findings

- **[medium]** Post-final requirement change with no listed source (frontmatter lines 3-5, 7-52; §7 lines 272-273) — Commit `8312bced` (2026-09-06) rewrote NFR3 and NFR4; `source_artifacts` lists no proposal for it, `updated` still says 2026-08-16, and the memlog has no entry between the 2026-08-16 finalize and this run. A reader cannot tell the new text was approved or where it came from (it appears to be back-ported from the Story 5.3 spec after 5.3 closed). *Fix:* Add a memlog `(override)` recovery entry, list the originating proposal or story spec in `source_artifacts`, and bump `updated`.
- **[low]** Foreign story numbering without qualification (§9.2 line 337 "Stories 22.7a-d") — These are Parties stories; the PRD never says so. *Fix:* "Parties Stories 22.7a-d".

## Downstream usability — adequate

Traceability is the PRD's strongest asset: §11.1 maps every FR to an epic with a one-line intent, §11.2 maps the high-risk NFRs to story IDs that all resolve in `epics.md` (verified through Story 7.20), and §11.3 names exact output paths. IDs are unique and stable. The four backlog artifact paths at lines 453-456 exist; the three spec paths at lines 449-451 do not yet, which is expected while Stories 6.1/6.3/6.5 are `backlog` (Story 6.1's frozen story spec exists as `spec-6-1-folded-snapshot-frozen-spec.md`, which is not the deliverable).

Two things degrade extraction. First, `epics.md`'s Requirements Inventory (lines 111 and 113) still carries the pre-2026-09-06 NFR3/NFR4 text while the PRD carries the rewrite and Story 5.3's acceptance criteria carry a third, fuller rendering — three wordings for two NFRs across the two artifacts the story pipeline reads. Second, the glossary (§4) has not grown with the requirements: FR27, FR36, FR37, §1.1, and §9.3 lean on OQ8, G5, goldens, parity packet, break-glass, provenance classification, `pdenc-v2`, durable admission, canonical-intent descriptor, retention tier, Parties, and corrective release, none defined.

### Findings

New text: nonempty asymmetric allowlist with no default, HTTPS required for authority/OIDC discovery, HS256-only symmetric, 60-second skew, enumerated secret kinds. A story cut from the inventory would be held to the weaker contract. *Fix:* Sync the inventory to the PRD text in one commit and add a Contracts-style guard that diffs PRD §7 against the inventory.
- **[medium]** "Story 8.6" is ambiguous between two numbering spaces (§6.8 line 254; §10 SM6 line 363) — "before Story 8.6 resumes" is unqualified in both places, but EventStore's own Epic 8 has Stories 8.1-8.11 (§9.3, §11.1 FR37 row), while the intended referent is the Parties story. §9.3 line 348 does qualify "Parties Story 8.7"; SM6 and §6.8 do not. *Fix:* Write "Parties Story 8.6" everywhere; consider a glossary entry for "Parties".
