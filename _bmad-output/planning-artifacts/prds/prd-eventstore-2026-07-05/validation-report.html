eventstore Phase 4 Implementation Readiness Recovery — Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prd.md · baseline 1b6f08d4
Poor

This is a technically substantive and unusually candid brownfield PRD: the thesis is specific, scope exclusions and residual risks are explicit, and most security, reliability, and evidence requirements carry product-specific bounds. It is not safe as the authoritative chain-top baseline it claims to be because its live FR ownership map contradicts its correction record, several omnibus requirements cannot close clause by clause, and readiness controls remain unbound or overdue.

The adversarial and brownfield reviewers shift the picture decisively to Reject. Four distinct critical failures survive consolidation: the FR ownership trace is self-contradictory, epics.md fails its own source-drift gate, OQ8 safety requirements depend on external normative bytes EventStore cannot reproduce, and the MVP metric can pass while a known concurrent append race still loses durable data. Do not use this PRD to authorize a READY verdict, MVP completion, consumer migration, or release.
