Hexalith.EventStore Admin — UX Design Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-eventstore-2026-07-05

The pair has a strong document shape and coherent operational model, but it is not yet a clean downstream contract. Its principal blockers are unbindable color tokens, contradictory source authority, and component/platform bindings that no longer commit to the published FrontComposer primitives; journey, state, and visual coverage are also incomplete.

The additional lenses materially raise the handoff risk. Architecture cannot safely consume a spine that cites an obsolete ready report beside the current blocked/reject baseline, while regulated/support-critical implementation needs explicit scope freezing, component semantics, refresh/retry controls, typed redaction, reflow, and evidence-expiry behavior. These are contract findings, not defects proven in the running product.

No overall grade is assigned. Raw reviewer counts: rubric 1 critical / 4 high / 9 medium / 6 low; accessibility 1 / 10 / 7 / 1; architecture readiness 1 / 6 / 5 / 2. Combined: 3 critical / 20 high / 21 medium / 9 low.

Accessibility & support-safety review

review-accessibility-support-safety.md

Thin and unsafe as the sole WCAG 2.2 AA implementation contract for regulated/support-critical use. Counts: 1 critical, 10 high, 7 medium, 1 low. The source file contains full impact analysis and closure evidence.

critical

Wrong-tenant destructive action is not adequately prevented

Fix: freeze and revalidate principal, environment, tenant, target, effect, reversibility, blast radius, and audit reason; prohibit cross-tenant bulk mutation.
high

Final spines are not revision-bound to current obligations

Fix: record source revisions/digests and validation identity, regenerate traceability, and close the current component/state/evidence matrix.
high

Named Fluent components lack exact semantic relationships

Fix: add a testable tab/accordion/grid/dialog/badge/toast semantics matrix.
high

Live announcements are not scoped, deduplicated, or throttled

Fix: define owning regions and announce only meaningful transitions.
high

Automatic polling has no pause, cadence, or state-preservation rule

Fix: add pause/resume and preserve focus, reading, selection, scroll, filters, expansion, and open dialogs.
high

Evidence timeout exposes unsafe retry/resubmission

Fix: separate status refresh from operation retry and fence retry on authoritative terminal evidence.
high

Sample/Tenants state closure is absent and states are conflated

Fix: add all canonical states per surface with focus, announcement, mutation, disclosure, and recovery rules.
high

Protected data is not assistive-technology-safe by contract

Fix: exclude sensitive bytes from DOM, accessibility properties, clipboard, URL, export, and telemetry; map typed outcomes safely.
high

Responsive behavior is breakpoint-only

Fix: require 320 CSS px, 400% zoom, text spacing, one-dimensional reflow, grid-region scrolling, and visible focus.
high

Promoted mocks make primary controls keyboard-inoperable

Fix: use semantic controls or annotate exact Fluent semantics and add visible-focus/narrow evidence.
high

Current evidence has no expiry or observation contract

Fix: require canonical source, observed-at/refresh times, freshness horizon and clock basis; stale or invalid evidence disables mutation.
medium

Focus movement and fallback targets are underdefined

Fix: define behavior for tabs, route entry, panels, disappearing rows, and validation.
medium

Non-text and forced-color contrast evidence is absent

Fix: require 3:1 and theme/forced-color checks for every load-bearing state.
medium

No target-size floor exists

Fix: require 24×24 CSS px or WCAG exception; prefer 44×44 for touch-critical controls.
medium

Reduced-motion rules omit shimmer, scrolling, and transitions

Fix: disable nonessential motion while preserving static progress/state information.
medium

Disabled safety reasons may be unreachable

Fix: render persistent associated explanation and an enabled safe next action.
medium

Accessible-only localization and support-safe formatting are omitted

Fix: resource announcements/names and test cultures, zones, plurals, pseudo-locales, RTL, identifiers, and truncation.
medium

Toasts are ungoverned and can imply false success

Fix: keep mutation evidence persistent; use neutral accepted copy and focus-safe, dismissible, deduplicated toasts.
low

data-testid is misplaced under Accessibility Floor

Fix: move it to testing and pair selectors with semantic assertions.

  • DESIGN and EXPERIENCE shapes satisfy their canonical section requirements.
