## Blocking gaps

- **G1 — Custody/physical provider:** complete and deliver the actual EXT-SECRETS-1 custody adapter and per-key lifecycle; production all-copy atomic manifest destruction with hold pins, immutable ordered receipts and exact original lookup. Authoritative discovery establishes OpenBao 2.6.2 with three Raft replicas and the later installed `ext-secrets-1-hmac` HMAC binding. Its mappings explicitly record ordinary snapshot semantics and `lifecycleOrAllCopyQualified:false`; no production DEK lifecycle or atomic manifest implementation is installed or present in Platform source. The missing provider architecture is a genuine decision, not an undocumented installation the user must identify. The [Platform decision and exact acceptance criteria](../../../platform/docs/implementation/ext-secrets-1-dek-provider-decision-2026-10-18.md) proposes a purpose-specific transactional OpenBao engine with separate lifecycle custody, or a separately selected transactional custody backend, each requiring independent all-copy/nonrollback proof. Neither is selected or qualified. Do not bind EventStore to the HMAC installation as a substitute.
- **G2 — Authority/trust:** implement actual published-trust ES256 deletion-capability validation, exact current online guard/dispatch/attestation validation, private authenticated compromise registrar and independent nonrollback transition authorities. An always-true callback or self-issued anchor is not acceptable.
- **G3 — Physical durability/restore:** qualify transaction atomicity, provider partial failure, every copy/TTL/backup/restore path, terminal receipts and revocation retention against the actual chosen provider.
- **G4 — Recovery/replay:** qualify actor-journal recovery with actual writer/anchor/scheduler authorities, supply mixed-root directory snapshot policy/bindings, and prove same-root source+target replay against the real installed engine. Source now includes durable idle completion reminders as well as original recovery and a distinct trusted production reader route; these are not physical/operational qualification. Interrupted preparation without a committed source journal still depends on independently proven NeverWritten recovery; the reminder must not infer that proof.
- **G5 — Installed owner fixture:** supply actual independently installed executor/observer/enrollment programs and complete low-level driver fixtures against the accepted source, provider and authority. No mock, Agents receiver or consuming runtime path may substitute.
- **G6 — Verifier completeness:** the 41-case harness now checks the actual deletion JWS, native canonical target digest, original batch/issue/dispatch records, native owner/provider ordered receipt vectors and their exact retry/lookup equality. Complete the remaining original hold, pin, coverage, re-attestation/activation, copy/restore and current-online-authority chains against real installations before acceptance as the register's complete executable verifier. Correlation and valid signatures alone do not prove native durability, linearizability, every copy's destruction or nonrollback.
- **G7 — Complete canonical target:** publication records this partial implementation only. No exact full delivery commit can be proposed until G1–G6 are closed and the complete implementation and verifier exist together at a target reachable from `origin/main`.
